Read-only account

Your account can see these worlds and download a version, but not check one out, check one in, or run the Artificer Companion — that needs the world custody grant. Ask whoever administers this vault to grant it to you; it takes them one click on the Users panel.

New shared world

Linking an installed game from the Artificer Companion also creates a world, with the game's details filled in — this form is the by-hand path.

Your companion

The Artificer Companion runs on your gaming machine: it finds installed games, links their save folders to worlds here, and moves the saves for you. The token is yours alone — minting a new one revokes the old everywhere. The download hands out the build this service ships, … — the companion shows its own version in its footer, so you can tell whether a download actually replaced the old one.

Users


World custody is the grant that lets someone check a world out, check it back in, and hold a companion token — without it an account can see the worlds and download a version, nothing more. New accounts made here get it. Accounts created by signing in through Cloudflare Access do not: Access says who someone is, never what they may do here, so grant custody below after their first sign-in. Admins have it implicitly, and additionally manage worlds, users and rollbacks.

Cover art (IGDB)

IGDB authenticates through a Twitch application, so the pair is a Twitch client id and secret. Saved here it is encrypted at rest and wins over IGDB_CLIENT_ID/IGDB_CLIENT_SECRET in the environment; removing it falls back to those. Companions never hold it — they ask this service for covers.

Save-location catalogue

Companions ask this service where a game keeps its saves, so no player's machine pulls the 17 MB catalogue. Data from the Ludusavi manifest (MIT), refreshed weekly. Without it, companions fall back to Steam Cloud paths and a folder search — fewer games get a suggested folder, and nothing else changes.
