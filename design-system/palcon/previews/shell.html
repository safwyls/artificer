Shell and Pal Spheres

A dark rail in a light app

The rail is the one dark surface here, and its servers are Pal Spheres: a conic ring split top-half in the server's assigned colour and bottom-half ink, like the game's capture sphere, with the server's initials in the middle and a connectivity dot on the rim. The dot re-probes on a timer, so a server that comes back stops looking offline without anyone refreshing.

Rules

  • The active sphere is at full opacity; the others sit at 60% and come up on hover. There is no second “active” treatment.
  • The rim dot is green when the server answered, ink-soft when it did not, and translucent while the probe is still out — three states, not two.
  • Switching servers keeps the current view: from a map, land on the new server's map.
