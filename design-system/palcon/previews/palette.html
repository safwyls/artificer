Palette

Warm paper and ink, brand red and amber, a deeper alarm red

The two-layer structure the consoles share, with one decision worth calling out: --destructive is not the brand red. It is a deeper, more alarm-toned red chosen specifically so a destructive verb stays visually distinct from a routine primary — on a page where the primary is already red, reusing it would make “Save” and “Delete” the same colour.

Rules

  • Destructive is deeper than the brand red. If they ever converge, kick and ban stop looking different from save.
  • The rail is ink — the one dark surface in a light app. It anchors the page and makes the spheres read.
  • Data colours are sampled, not chosen: the passive-tier chips take their hues from the game's own tier icons, and the per-stat tints are fixed in lib/stats.ts. Re-theming the console must not re-theme the data.
  • --radius is 0.85rem. Palcon is the round one; wildskeeper is 6px with a notch, flametender is 4px square.
